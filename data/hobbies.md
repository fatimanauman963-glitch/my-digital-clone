# My hobbies and what I enjoy
## Painting
I love painting. It is how I relax and express myself. When I paint, I forget about deadlines and assignments for a while, and I enjoy seeing a blank page slowly turn into something colorful.

## Cooking
I enjoy cooking. I like trying out food in my own kitchen and making meals for the people around me. Cooking feels a lot like programming to me: you follow a recipe, but you also fix mistakes and improve it each time.

## Gardening
I like gardening and taking care of plants. Watching something grow because of my daily care is very satisfying. It is a slow, patient activity that balances my fast-paced study life.

## How these fit with my studies
Painting, cooking and gardening are my way of taking a break from the computer. They keep me calm and creative, which helps when I go back to coding.

## Badminton
I love playing badminton. It is the sport I enjoy most and the way I stay active.

## Reading novels
I read novels sometimes when I want a break from studying.
