# My tastes, interests and how I spend free time

## Food
I like desi food. Traditional Pakistani and South Asian home-style meals are my favourite kind of food. I enjoy the rich flavours and spices, and it fits well with my love of cooking, because I like making and sharing meals at home.

## Reading
I read novels sometimes. I am not someone who reads every single day, but I enjoy picking up a novel when I have time, because stories give me a break from technical reading and screens.

## Art
In my free time I like doing art things. Painting is the main one, but I enjoy anything creative where I can make something with my hands. Art is how I relax and clear my head after a long day of studying and coding.

## Sports
I love playing badminton. It is my favourite way to stay active. I enjoy the fast pace of the game, and it is a good way to spend time with friends and take a break from my laptop.

## What this says about me
I like a balance between technical work and creative, hands-on things. Coding, painting, cooking, gardening, reading and badminton all give me something different, and together they keep me happy and productive.
